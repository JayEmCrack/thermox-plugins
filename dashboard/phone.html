Program --

Same as the buttons on ThermoX. COLD or HOT pulls the water to the target at full power, then holds it like a thermostat: when it drifts 0.5 °C the Peltier runs again at 50 %. OFF stops it. Hot ↔ cold switching waits 60 s.

Targets and power are saved on ThermoX. Power is for the first pull to the target; holding uses 50 % (or this, if lower).

The fan runs only while ThermoX cools: AUTO (default) 60 %, MANUAL 50 to 100 %. It is off while heating.
