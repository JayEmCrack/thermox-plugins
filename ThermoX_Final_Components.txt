ESP32 PIN MAP (ThermoX.ino)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- GPIO18 Button 1 (DOWN)          - GPIO33 Button 2 (UP)
- GPIO21 OLED SDA                 - GPIO22 OLED SCL
- GPIO26 BTS7960 RPWM             - GPIO27 BTS7960 LPWM
- GPIO25 BTS7960 R_EN             - GPIO13 BTS7960 L_EN
- GPIO14 DS18B20 data             - GPIO23 Fan PWM
- GPIO35 Fan tach (FG)            - GPIO36 (VP) Battery voltage
- GPIO19 Charger detect           - GPIO32 and GPIO34: not used

IMPORTANT SETTINGS / SAFETY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Trigger board: 12 V only (never 15 V or 20 V); measure before connecting
- LM2596S: set to 5.0 V with a meter before wiring it to the board
- F1 10 A in the main 12 V line, F2 3 A in the BAT+ wire to the board (close to the battery)
- Only one charger at a time: never plug the USB-C and the DC charger in together
- Charge with SW1 in the middle (OFF); ThermoX is locked while charging
- ThermoX.ino: set ENABLE_BATTERY_SENSE = true (the battery divider is on the board)
- Use a 12.6 V (3S) charger only, never 21 V
- Water cutoffs: 55 C and 5 C; a stalled fan stops the Peltier
- Not built and tested yet: lay the parts on the board and check each step with a meter
